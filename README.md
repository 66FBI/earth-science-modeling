# Earth Science Modeling

A collection of computational modeling and numerical simulation exercises developed in **Python and R**, covering physical processes, stochastic simulation, parallel and GPU computing, Monte Carlo methods, and Bayesian analysis.

The repository contains nine notebooks exploring different approaches to mathematical and computational modeling, ranging from numerical solutions of differential equations to seismic wave propagation and climate trend analysis.

## Notebooks

### 1. [Laplace Equation](notebooks/01_laplace_equation.ipynb)

Iterative numerical solution of a two-dimensional Laplace-type problem on a regular grid.

The notebook explores:

- definition of boundary conditions,
- iterative grid updates,
- convergence of the numerical solution,
- visualization of the evolving two-dimensional field.

### 2. [Diffusion and Random Walk](notebooks/02_diffusion_random_walk.ipynb)

Simulation of diffusion and stochastic movement using random-walk processes.

The notebook includes:

- two-dimensional diffusion,
- random movement of a source,
- stochastic trajectories,
- Lévy-type jumps,
- visualization of spatial diffusion processes.

### 3. [Heat Diffusion](notebooks/03_heat_diffusion.ipynb)

Numerical simulation of heat diffusion in a heterogeneous medium.

The model investigates:

- spatial heat propagation,
- spatially varying material properties,
- heterogeneous regions and inclusions,
- evolution of the temperature field,
- convergence toward a steady-state solution.

### 4. [Gravity Modeling and Parallelization](notebooks/04_gravity_modeling_parallelization.ipynb)

Numerical modeling of the gravitational response generated by a synthetic subsurface density distribution.

The exercise combines physical modeling with computational performance analysis through:

- construction of a synthetic density model,
- calculation of its gravitational response,
- parallel execution of numerical calculations,
- benchmarking computation time for different numbers of workers.

### 5. [Seismic Wave Propagation](notebooks/05_seismic_wave_propagation.ipynb)

Finite-difference simulation of seismic wave propagation through a two-layer medium.

The notebook includes:

- spatially varying wave velocity,
- a Ricker wavelet source,
- finite-difference field updates,
- absorbing boundary treatment,
- visualization of wave propagation,
- recording of a synthetic seismogram.

### 6. [Football League Monte Carlo Simulation](notebooks/06_football_league_monte_carlo.ipynb)

Monte Carlo simulation of possible outcomes for the remaining matches of a football league season.

Repeated simulations are used to estimate the probability of different final outcomes, including:

- league position,
- promotion,
- championship,
- relegation.

The exercise demonstrates how stochastic simulation can be applied to a discrete real-world system outside traditional Earth science applications.

### 7. [Wave Propagation and Geometry](notebooks/07_wave_propagation_geometry.ipynb)

Comparison of wave propagation through two different geometric configurations.

Two variants are modeled:

- geometry with sharp corners,
- geometry with rounded transitions.

The simulations are compared to investigate how geometry influences wave reflection, energy concentration and maximum pressure.

### 8. [GPU-Accelerated Wave Simulation](notebooks/08_gpu_accelerated_wave_simulation.ipynb)

GPU-accelerated numerical wave simulation using **CuPy**.

The notebook extends earlier wave-propagation experiments by implementing computations on the GPU and comparing their performance with CPU-based NumPy calculations.

The analysis includes:

- vectorized numerical simulation,
- heterogeneous material properties,
- Ricker wavelet excitation,
- GPU computation with CuPy,
- CPU/GPU execution-time comparison,
- performance benchmarking across multiple grid sizes.

### 9. [MCMC Climate Trend Analysis](notebooks/09_mcmc_climate_trend_analysis.ipynb)

Bayesian parameter estimation and climate trend analysis using the **Metropolis–Hastings Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC)** algorithm.

The notebook explores:

- likelihood functions,
- prior and posterior distributions,
- proposal distributions,
- MCMC sampling,
- burn-in,
- acceptance rates,
- posterior parameter distributions,
- comparison with conventional linear regression,
- application to historical climate observations.
